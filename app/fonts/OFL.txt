Font attribution
=================

Cover Coast ships three fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License
version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org):

- Anton, by Vernon Adams — app/fonts/Anton-Regular.ttf
  Used for the big poster title.
- Pacifico, by Vernon Adams — app/fonts/Pacifico-Regular.ttf
  Used for the script subtitle.
- Bebas Neue, by Ryoichi Tsunekawa (Dharma Type) — app/fonts/BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
  Used for the badge ribbon lettering.

Each font is free to use, study, copy and redistribute under the terms
of the SIL Open Font License, including use inside this app and in the
covers it exports. No font file here comes from Rockstar Games or any
game; all three are open-licensed display fonts.
