Cover Coast — font attribution
================================

The three fonts bundled under app/fonts/ are released under the
SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which allows them to be bundled,
used and redistributed with this app:

- Anton (Anton-Regular.ttf) — a bold condensed sans, used for the cover title.
- Pacifico (Pacifico-Regular.ttf) — a script face, used for the subtitle.
- Bebas Neue (BebasNeue-Regular.ttf) — a tall condensed sans, used for the badge ribbon.

No Rockstar Games font, logo, wordmark or artwork is used in this app.
The full SIL Open Font License text is available at
https://openfontlicense.org/ (OFL-1.1).
